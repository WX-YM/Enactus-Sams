01 — Who we are

A club that builds businesses to solve problems.

Enactus is a global network of students, academics and business leaders. Our chapter at Sadat Academy — Maadi runs the full cycle ourselves: we find a problem in our community, design a venture that answers it, build it, measure it, and defend it in front of national judges.

Nobody here is only a member. You write, you pitch, you film, you negotiate, you manage a budget — usually in the same week.

02 — This year's project

Tafrah.

طفـــرة — the leap

A training and employment platform built for autistic people in Egypt: a calm working environment, direct instruction, and real job opportunities that protect their rights.
